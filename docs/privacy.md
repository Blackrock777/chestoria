# CheStoria Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

## Exploring as a guest

You can use the guide, stories, maps, camera recognition, and stickers without an account. CheStoria does not sell your personal information, track you across apps or websites, or include advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Camera, photos and location

CheStoria asks to use the camera when you enable it. You can also select an image with Apple’s photo picker. Recognition runs on your device. CheStoria does not upload camera images, selected photos, or camera frames. The app can keep a resized copy of a matched photo on the device as part of your sticker collection. When you choose to share a postcard, the app hands it to the destination you select in the system share sheet.

Location helps suggest nearby landmarks and narrow photo matches. CheStoria does not send your location to a CheStoria server. On Apple Watch, location may continue in the background during a walk you start, for up to two hours. You can end the walk at any time. The phone sends discovered landmark IDs and app preferences to your paired Watch. Directions open Apple Maps, and source or ticket links open the sites you choose to visit.

You can change camera, location and notification permissions in system settings. The map remains available to guests without those permissions.

## Optional accounts

In this release, accounts are stored in the Keychain on your iPhone. If you create an email account, CheStoria stores your name, email address and a salted password hash. For Sign in with Apple, the app keeps your Apple account identifier for CheStoria and any name or email address Apple shares with it. This release does not send your account information to a CheStoria server. Apple handles the Sign in with Apple authorization and the system services you choose to use.

## Retention and deletion

Your trip progress, sticker photos and preferences stay on the device. Erasing trip data or removing the app clears the app’s saved trip. In Profile, **Erase trip data** clears saved trip progress, sticker photos and any recall data from earlier versions on this iPhone. The cleared trip is sent to the paired Watch when it reconnects. Account deletion also removes your CheStoria account record and erases the trip. After deleting a Sign in with Apple account, you can remove CheStoria’s authorization in your Apple Account settings. Copies you export or share are managed by the destination you select.

## Contact

Use the support link in CheStoria’s App Store listing for privacy questions or help with your account. Keep account details and photos out of public issue reports.
