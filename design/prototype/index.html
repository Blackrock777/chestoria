    Thermopolium · Pompeii

    Romans queued at this counter every day. What was it?

    How sure?
    You said bank. Nope! Good guess though.

    A street-food bar.

    Romans called it a thermopolium.

    The holes held big jars of hot food and wine, served straight to people in the street. This one belonged to Lucius Vetutius Placidus.

    ✓ Verified · Pompeii Archaeological Park
